# Royal Rudraksh Palace - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## Property Ownership & Management
- **Q: Who is the owner of Royal Rudraksh Palace?**
  **A:** Royal Rudraksh Palace is owned and operated by Mr Sudhir Kumar Sharma and the Rudraksh Hospitality Group. The property management team is dedicated to providing luxury accommodations, world-class hospitality, and memorable guest experiences.

## Location & Transportation
- **Q: Where is Royal Rudraksh Palace located?**
  **A:**Royal Rudraksh Palace is conveniently located right on the National Highway (NH), approximately 3-4 km from the airport area. While it is situated away from the busy city, its prime highway location provides easy and accessible transport options to and from the city.
- **Q: Is airport shuttle service available?**
  **A:**Yes, private airport or railway pick-up and drop-off services are available upon request for an additional fee based on current market cab price. Guests should contact the support desk at least 24 hours prior to arrival to schedule transfers
- **Q: Is parking available on-site?**
  **A:** Yes, complimentary valet parking is available for all registered hotel guests in our secure parking outside/inside facility.

## Amenities & Services
- **Q: What are the dining options at Royal Rudraksh Palace?**
  **A:** We offer a dedicated 24*7 in-house dining service for our guests. While there is no traditional dine-in restaurant on the property, all meals and beverages are available on a pre-booking basis and are delivered freshly prepared directly to your room by our service team.
- **Q: Is Wi-Fi complimentary?**
  **A:** Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi is complimentary for all guests throughout the rooms and public areas.
- **Q: Are swimming pool and gym facilities available?**
  **A:** We do not have a swimming pool, gym, or spa on the property. Our primary focus is on providing a peaceful, comfortable, and highly personalized room stay.
  **Q: Is power backup available at the property?**
  **A:** Yes, the property is equipped with 24/7 power backup to ensure an uninterrupted and comfortable stay.
  **Q: What ID proofs are accepted for check-in?**
  **A:** Guests must provide a valid government-issued photo ID (such as an Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport). Please note that PAN cards are not accepted for check-in.
