# Royal Rudraksh Palace - Hotel Policies

## Check-In & Check-Out
- **Check-In Time**: 10:00 AM (10:00) IST.
- **Check-Out Time**: 09:00 AM (09:00) IST.
- **Early Check-In**: Subject to availability upon arrival. Guaranteed early check-in requires booking the previous night.
- **Late Check-Out**: Subject to availability. Check-out between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM incurs a 50% room rate charge. Check-out after 4:00 PM incurs full night charge.
- **Government ID Requirement**: All guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID (Passport, Aadhaar Card, Driving License, Voter ID) at check-in. PAN Card is not accepted as address proof per government regulations.

## Cancellation & Modification Policy
- **Standard Refundable Rate**: Free cancellation and modification up to 48 hours before check-in time (2:00 PM on arrival date).
- **Late Cancellation / No-Show**: Cancellations made within 48 hours of check-in or no-shows will be charged the equivalent of 1 night's room stay plus taxes.
- **Non-Refundable Rate Bookings**: Non-refundable bookings cannot be cancelled or modified for a refund.

## Payment Policies
- **Accepted Payment Methods**: Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), Debit Cards, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), NetBanking, and Cash.
- **Security Deposit**: A security deposit of ₹2,000 per room per night or credit card pre-authorization is required at check-in for incidental charges.

## Child & Extra Bed Policy
- **Children (0-5 years)**: Stay for free when using existing bedding.
- **Children (6-12 years)**: Additional charge of ₹1,000 per night per child including breakfast.
- **Extra Bed / Adults (13+ years)**: ₹1,500 per night including rollaway bed and breakfast.

## Pet Policy
- Pets are strictly not allowed on hotel premises, with the exception of registered service animals with prior notification.

## Smoking & Alcohol Policy
- All guest rooms, corridors, and indoor public spaces are 100% non-smoking. A cleaning fee of ₹5,000 will be assessed for smoking inside guest rooms.
- Designated smoking areas are located in the outdoor garden.
- Alcohol consumption is not permitted in any part of the hotel.
